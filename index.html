Why Bears Enter Markets

Understanding cause and effect is key to coexistence.

  • Attractants: Open garbage and food waste release strong scents that travel miles.
  • Habitat Fragmentation: Traditional corridors are blocked by new construction.
  • Easy Calories: High-calorie human food is easier to obtain than foraging.
